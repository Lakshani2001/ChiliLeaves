Our Story

ChiliGuard AI was founded by a team of agricultural scientists and AI engineers passionate about helping farmers protect their crops. With chili being one of the world's most important spice crops, disease management is critical to ensuring food security and farmer livelihoods.

Our AI model was trained on over 50,000+ labeled images of chili leaves across 12+ disease categories, achieving a detection accuracy of 95%+ in independent tests.
